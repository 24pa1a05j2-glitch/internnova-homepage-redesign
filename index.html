GOOD QUESTIONS

Things you might
want to know.

Have a question before you get started? Here are a few useful details to check.

?
Need more information?Visit InternNova's official pages for current program details.Visit official website ↗
Do I need prior experience to apply?

Requirements vary by program. Review the current program description to understand prerequisites and the skills you should have before applying.

Are the internships online?

InternNova describes its platform as offering virtual internship experiences. Check the details for your selected program and intake before enrolling.

How long does a program last?

Duration can differ between programs and cohorts. Visit the official internships page for the latest schedule and expected commitment.

Will I work on practical projects?

Project-based tasks are part of InternNova's described learning approach. Check the selected program's curriculum for specific assignments and final project requirements.

Can I receive a certificate?

Certificate availability and completion criteria depend on the program. Confirm the exact requirements with InternNova before applying.

How do I apply for a program?

Browse available programs on the official website, choose a track, and follow the application instructions shown for the current intake.
